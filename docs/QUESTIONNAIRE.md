# Inputs needed to finish each agent

We fill this one agent at a time. Answers go into `context/` and each agent's file.

## 0 · Foundations (all agents)
1. What Osmossi sells: categories, price range, hero products; store URL(s); Shopify vs Lovable role.
2. Brand voice: 3-5 adjectives, language per channel, words to use / avoid, 2-3 example posts.
3. Audience: age, US states/metros, communities, self-purchase vs gifting, main buying objections.
4. Goals: monthly revenue / orders / traffic targets and today's baseline.
5. Approval gates: who approves publishing, spend, terms, prices; any no-approval spend limit.

## 1 · Market Intelligence Agent
1. Top 3-5 competitors (site + Instagram handle) and 2-3 brands you admire.
2. Trend sources: Instagram accounts, Pinterest boards, hashtags to watch.
3. Holiday / moment calendar beyond US holidays (Día de las Madres, quinceañeras, Navidad...).
4. Which products to price-benchmark, and target margin / price position (premium, mid, value).
5. Cadence and output: weekly brief? Save trends into the ERP Trends board automatically?

## 2 · Growth Planner Agent
1. How you plan today: month plan / week focus in the ERP, who writes it.
2. Rules for picking hero products (margin, stock, seasonality, newness).
3. Bundles / gift guides: allowed discount depth, minimum margin.
4. Monthly marketing budget and organic vs paid split.

## 3 · Content Studio Agent
1. Canva brand kit name; SharePoint "Leales" folder path for photos.
2. "Osmossi grade": describe the photo look (or point to reference images).
3. Reel style: length, music, captions, on-camera people vs product-only.
4. Pinterest: boards that exist, pin formats.
5. Hashtags / CTAs we always use.

## 4 · Distribution & Ads Agent
1. Posting cadence per channel and best posting times.
2. Meta Ads: account access, pixel installed?, typical audiences, UTM convention.
3. Creators: tiers you work with, what you offer (product, fee, commission).
4. Lead capture: email tool (Shopify Email? Klaviyo?), WhatsApp Business number, lead magnet.

## 5 · Store Conversion Agent
1. Which site is the money page (Shopify vs Lovable) and who can edit it.
2. Offers available: installments (Shop Pay / Afterpay?), free shipping threshold, returns.
3. Reviews app; abandoned-checkout flow today.
4. DM sales: who answers DMs today, tone, what the assistant may and may not promise.

## 6 · Insights Agent
1. KPIs that matter (top 5) and their targets.
2. Access: GA4, Meta Ads reporting, Lovable analytics.
3. Report day/time and where it goes (ERP, email, Teams).

## 7 · Loyalty Agent
1. Post-purchase timing and channel; handwritten note / insert?
2. Review incentive rules; UGC rights wording.
3. Repeat / referral offer: what reward, how often.

## Marketing Lead (orchestrator)
1. Weekly rhythm: which day each stage runs.
2. Who receives the weekly hand-off and in what form.
