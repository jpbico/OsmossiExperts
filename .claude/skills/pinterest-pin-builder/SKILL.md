## Steps
1. Reuse the best feed or carousel photos (no new shoot). Make **2–3 Pins per product** with
   different photos or titles.
2. Design at 1000×1500 from the brand kit: a short overlay that names a benefit or idea
   ("Handwoven bag for holiday nights"), small logo, Beige Sand / Green.
3. Copy from `brand-voice-copy` (title under 100 characters, keyword description, no emojis).
4. Link: `https://www.osmossi.com/product/<handle>?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=<yyyy-mm-theme>`.
5. Seasonal Pins go up **at least 4–6 weeks before** the moment (Pinterest surfaces them slowly).
