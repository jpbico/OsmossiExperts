# audience-insights

Owner agent: `market-intelligence`. Runs monthly (first week) or on request.

## Inputs
- `context/audience.md` (current profile and objections)
- Public comments and reviews on competitor sites and posts; Pinterest and search results;
  published research on Hispanic consumers in the US

## Steps
1. Collect what buyers say in their own words about woven and beaded bags: reviews, comments,
   questions. Note the language (Spanish, English, Spanglish) they use.
2. Group the findings under the two known objections, **"it's common"** and **"it's not
   stylish"**, plus any new ones (price, shipping, authenticity, care).
3. For each objection, note what answers it: proof, styling, story, guarantee.
4. Note differences by community (for example, Colombian buyers may already know Wayuu bags).

## Output format
- An "Audience" section in the brief: 3–5 insights, each a quote or data point with its source,
  and the content angle it suggests.
- Proposed changes to `context/audience.md`, shown as a diff for Juan Pablo to accept.

## Rules
- Quote public text only, never names or handles of private people.
- Do not edit `context/audience.md` without approval.
