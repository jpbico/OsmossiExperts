# reel-editor

Owner agent: `content-studio`.

## Inputs
- Clips: SharePoint `4 - Marketing\Biblioteca generar contenido` (e.g. "Mujeres tejiendo.mp4").
- The ERP brief (hook, scenes, emotion, CTA, music mood). Standards: `context/brand.md`.

## Steps
1. Do **not** use Canva `create-video`: it generates AI footage, not an edit of our clips.
   Build a 9:16 storyboard design with `create-design` (format "Instagram Story", brand kit
   `kAHOo0oQgAk`), one page per scene, from real product photos imported from Shopify's CDN.
2. **Plan the timeline** before building: a table of time, clip, on-screen text, and note.
   - 0–2 s: hook (detail close-up, motion, reveal, or on-screen question).
   - Product showcase: cut every 1–3 s. Artisan or process story: slower, lingering shots.
   - Structure: hook → product/detail → context (where it's from, how it's worn) → CTA.
   - Last 2–3 s: logo as supplied + "Shop at osmossi.com".
3. In the timeline, mark which pages are ready and which need a clip or SharePoint photo added
   by Andrea (file name, folder, and the seconds to use). On-screen text in Playfair Display /
   Lora, inside the safe zone, on every key moment. Logo only on the end card.
4. **Cover**: a clear, centered frame that also reads in the 4:5 grid crop.
5. **Music**: name a mood (warm Latin acoustic, soft cumbia, bossa, tender bolero
   instrumental) and remind Andrea to pick the track in Instagram's library. Never add music
   inside Canva for Instagram posts.
6. Make a **video Pin** cut (6–15 s, title overlay in the first seconds) when the plan has one.
7. Offer **two hooks** (two opening scenes or two first lines).

## What Andrea finishes
Adding clips, page timings, transitions and the MP4 export happen in Canva by hand. Say exactly
what is left; never present the storyboard as a finished Reel.
