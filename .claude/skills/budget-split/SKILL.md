# budget-split

Owner agent: `growth-planner`.

## Budget (agreed 2026-11-19)
- **Paid:** $5/day (about $150/month) for boosting posts. Each boost campaign needs Juan
  Pablo's yes.
- **Organic:** everything else (content, Pinterest, Stories, creators via product only, unless
  approved otherwise).
- Small spends under $30 each, up to $100/week, need no approval (`context/approval-gates.md`).

## Steps
1. **What to boost:** only posts already proven organically. In the first 48 hours, a boost
   candidate must be in the top third of recent posts for saves or link taps. Never boost a
   post that didn't work organically.
2. **When:** the 2–3 weeks before a gifting moment (the purchase weeks of the funnel).
3. **Who:** women 25–55 in Miami–Fort Lauderdale–Boca Raton and Los Angeles, interested in
   fashion, handbags, handmade and Latin culture; English and Spanish. Destination: the
   product page or gift guide with UTMs.
4. **How long:** 7-day boosts at $5/day, so one variable changes at a time.
5. **Stop rule:** pause a boost after 3 days if link taps are below 0.5% of reach.
