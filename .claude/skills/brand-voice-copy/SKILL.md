# brand-voice-copy

Owner agent: `content-studio`.

## Inputs
- The ERP post brief (goal, emotion, CTA, keywords), the product (ERP `search_products`), the
  week focus, and `context/brand.md`.

## Instagram caption
1. **Hook line**: one short line that stops the scroll (a feeling, a question, a detail).
2. **2–4 short lines**: craft and roots, then the occasion or the styling. Answer one of her
   objections: show it is special (handmade in small batches, the hands behind it) or that it
   is stylish (how she wears it now).
3. **CTA**: the brief's CTA (shop, save, tag her, comment). Shop CTAs say "link in bio" in feed
   posts and use a link sticker in Stories.
4. **Hashtags**: 3–5, niche + broad, from `context/market-watchlist.md` plus #Osmossi.
5. **Emojis**: up to two, elegant and polite (see `context/brand.md`).
6. Write **two options** (A and B) with different hooks or tones.

## Alt text
One or two plain sentences describing what is in the image: product, color, material, setting.
No hashtags or selling.

## Pinterest
- **Title**: under 100 characters, the search phrase first ("Handwoven Wayuu bum bag with
  beadwork | Osmossi"). No emojis.
- **Description**: 2–3 natural sentences using keywords people search ("handmade gift for her",
  "Colombian handwoven bag"), then "Shop at osmossi.com".
- **Board**: from `pinterest-pin-builder`.

## Words
- Use: handwoven, hand-beaded, handmade in small batches, Wayuu artisans, Colombian
  craftsmanship, curated, roots, timeless, detail.
- Never: "one of a kind", "last one", "we make", "cheap", "deal", "sale" (no blanket discounts),
  invented artisan facts, stereotypes or clichés about Latin American culture.
