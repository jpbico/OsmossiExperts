# Osmossi: audience

**Primary buyer:** a Latina woman living in the US, **20–60 years old**, who buys mainly
**for herself** and also as a gift.

- **Where she lives:** South Florida (Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton), Los Angeles, and
  similar metros with large Latin American communities.
- **Home countries we speak to most:** Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Puerto Rico, Mexico.
- **What stops her from buying:**
  1. The product feels **common**: she has seen woven bags everywhere.
  2. It doesn't look **stylish** enough to wear in her life in the US.
- **So every message must prove** the piece is special (handmade in small batches, the artisan
  and the hours behind it) *and* that it is fashion she can wear now (styled looks, not souvenirs).
