# Human approval gates

Agents prepare; a partner approves.

| Gate | Who approves |
|------|--------------|
| Publishing any post, pin, reel, email or ad | **Andrea** |
| Content approval (copy, design) | **Andrea** |
| Spending money (ads, creators, paid tools, margin-costing discounts) | **Juan Pablo**. Spend **under $30** may go ahead without approval. |
| Accepting terms (data providers, creator agreements, platform policies) | **Juan Pablo** |
| Price changes | **Juan Pablo** |
| Anything else that is unclear | **Juan Pablo** (decision maker) |

The under-$30 exception is per item, not per week: several small spends that add up to $30 or
