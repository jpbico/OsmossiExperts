# Human approval gates

Agents prepare; a partner approves.

| Gate | Who approves |
|------|--------------|
| Publishing any post, pin, reel, email or ad | **Andrea** |
| Content approval (copy, design) | **Andrea** |
| Spending money (ads, creators, paid tools, margin-costing discounts) | **Juan Pablo**. A purchase **under $30** may go ahead without approval, up to **$100 per week** in total. |
| Accepting terms (data providers, creator agreements, platform policies) | **Juan Pablo** |
| Price changes | **Juan Pablo** |
| Anything else that is unclear | **Juan Pablo** (decision maker) |

Small spends: under $30 per purchase, and no more than $100 per week (Monday–Sunday) of such
spends without approval. Track them in the weekly hand-off.

**Paid boost budget:** $5/day is the agreed starting budget for paid boosts. A boost still needs
Juan Pablo's yes per campaign (it is a recurring spend, not a single purchase under $30).

Writing research into the ERP (for example, trend notes) is not publishing and needs no approval.
