# Human approval gates

Agents prepare; a partner approves. These always need an explicit yes:

1. **Publishing** any post, pin, reel, email or ad.
2. **Spending money**: ad budgets, creator fees, paid tools, discounts that cost margin.
3. **Accepting terms**: data-provider terms, creator agreements, platform policies.
4. **Price changes** on any product.
