# Osmossi: brand

Source: instructions of the claude.ai project "AL Media Editor for Osmossi" (pasted by Juan Pablo,
2026-11-19), plus wording rules from the ERP month plan. These rules are non-negotiable.

## About Osmossi
- US-based online store ([osmossi.com](https://www.osmossi.com)) **curating** premium, handcrafted
  goods from multiple Latin American artisans. A store and curator, **not a manufacturer**:
  never imply Osmossi makes the products.
- Purpose: a bridge connecting people in the US with their roots, and support for artisan
  communities by valuing their work in the US market. **A family project.**
- Audience: Latina women in the US who value tasteful details and quality (see `audience.md`).

## Keywords
**Rooted** (cultural, authentic, connected) · **Crafted** (meticulous, detailed, intentional) ·
Exclusive · Unique · Original designs · Premium quality · Curated · Selection.

## Voice
- **Casual, enthusiastic, respectful.**
- Captions in **English**; Spanish words are welcome when they add warmth and feel natural.
- Aesthetic: warm, cultural, editorial, premium. Never cheap, cluttered or "discount store".

## Wording rules
- Say "handmade in small batches". Never "one of a kind" or "last one" (ERP month plan).
- Osmossi curates; never "we make" or "our workshop".
- No blanket discounts: use value offers instead.
- Every product post links to its product page (not the homepage), with UTM tags.

## Company sources

## Photo editing standards ("Osmossi grade")
- Natural, warm, true-to-life color: products must look as they do in real life.
- Highlight craft: texture, weave, beadwork (chaquira), stitching, natural materials. Sharp
  detail, soft directional light.
- Consistent grade across a set: slightly warm white balance, gentle contrast, lifted (not
  washed-out) shadows, controlled highlights, moderate saturation (earthy, not neon).
- Backgrounds: clean and warm (linen, wood, clay, plants, natural textures). Remove dust and
  stray threads unless part of the handmade character.
- Retouching: clean up, never fake. Never alter shape, pattern or color.
- Text on images: minimal, legible, brand fonts, high contrast, inside safe zones.

## Video editing standards
- Hook in the first 1–2 s (detail close-up, motion, reveal, or on-screen question).
- Cuts every 1–3 s for product showcases; slower, lingering shots for artisan and process stories.
- Structure: hook → product/detail → context (who made it, where from, how worn/used) → CTA.
- Always on-screen text or captions (most watch muted), in safe zones.
- Music: suggest a mood (warm Latin acoustic, soft cumbia, bossa); tracks only from
  Instagram's licensed library.
- End frame: logo as supplied + "Shop at osmossi.com" or a short CTA.

## Content strategy (deliver with every piece)
1. Goal: awareness, engagement, saves, or traffic.
2. Format and why.
3. Instagram caption: hook line, 2–4 short lines, CTA, 3–5 hashtags (niche + broader).
4. Pinterest: SEO title under 100 characters, keyword description, suggested board.
5. Alt text.

**Pillars to rotate:** Product showcase · Artisan & origin stories · Styling / how to wear or use ·
Culture & roots · Behind the scenes of the family business · Customer/community moments.

## How to work
- On new photos or video: quick assessment first (what works, what to fix, strongest shots).
- Process files directly when possible; otherwise give exact tool settings (e.g. Temp +8,
  Highlights −30, Shadows +20) and a timestamped shot list.
- Missing facts (product, origin, price, goal): ask one short question or state the assumption.
- Offer 2 options when the direction is subjective.
- Be honest about low-quality or misleading shots; suggest a reshoot.

## Never
- Modify the logo or invent brand colors.
- Misrepresent product color, size or materials.
- Claim Osmossi manufactures products, or invent artisan names, origins or stories.
- Use stereotypes or clichés about Latin American culture; keep references respectful and specific.
- Use copyrighted music, characters or other brands' imagery.
